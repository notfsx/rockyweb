protection

The default mode applies to every website that doesn't have its own. Change a single site from the toolbar popup.

default mode

how hard Rockyweb works

Your explicit choices always come first, then the mode for each site, then Light, then automatic optimisation. Light never undoes a choice you made.

automatically optimise protection for each site

Uses Chrome's on-device AI for small adjustments: hiding an unsolicited newsletter overlay on an article, which you can undo from the toolbar, and answering a cookie banner Rockyweb does not recognise by clicking its refuse button when your cookie choices refuse something. For that, the banner's heading and button labels are given to the on-device model. Nothing leaves your device. A banner answer is listed in the toolbar; it cannot be unclicked, but the site's own cookie settings link reopens its choices.

let AI change pages, not only settings

With this on, "describe a change" in the toolbar can also hide, unstick, resize and recolour parts of a page, from a list of safe changes. You see it on the page before you apply it. Every change is saved for that website, so you never have to ask twice, and listed in the toolbar and under page changes, where you can switch it off or delete it.

page fixes

cookies

Your answer to every cookie popup. Rockyweb uses the popup provider's own controls when it can, otherwise clicks the right buttons, then unlocks pages left unscrollable. Strict mode always rejects everything optional.

custom mode

What the custom default mode turns on. Changing one of these while another mode is the default makes custom the default, starting from that mode's settings.

websites

Sites with their own mode, domain choices or hidden elements. A setting covers the whole domain, so shop.example.com uses example.com.

trackers

Third-party domains Rocky has sniffed out. One that follows you to 3 or more websites is learned: balanced strips its cookies and strict blocks it. Payment, login and captcha providers are never restricted automatically, and shared CDNs at most lose their cookies.

reset learning

Forget every learned tracker. Your own choices for individual domains are kept.

page changes

Elements you hid with the picker (popup, gear, hide an element) or added here. They stay hidden on that site in every mode except off.

described changes and page fixes

Changes you described in the toolbar, and page fixes you applied or Rockyweb applied for you. Switching one off puts the page, and any setting it changed, back as it was.

data

statistics

Counted on this device only.

export

Every setting, website and learned tracker as rockyweb-settings.json.

import

Replaces your current settings. Every field is checked before it is used.

automatic adjustment memory

Decisions remembered for a week and undos remembered for 30 days, per website.

reset everything

Back to balanced. Forgets all websites, trackers, hidden elements, automatic adjustments and statistics.
