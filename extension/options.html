rocky's on guard

Rockyweb is already working on every website. There is nothing you have to set up.

one optional extra: on-device AI experimental

Chrome can run a small AI model on this computer. With it, Rockyweb can make page changes you describe in the toolbar ("hide the sticky header and make the text bigger"), offer fixes for common page annoyances, and refuse cookie banners its rules don't recognise. It never accepts cookies for you, every change can be undone, and the model runs on your device.

It is new and can be slow: the first answer after Chrome starts can take several minutes while Chrome loads the model.

Change your answer to cookie popups under cookies, or see all settings. The toolbar icon has a switch for each website.

Rockyweb is open source: github.com/notfsx/rockyweb

cookies

Your answer to every cookie popup. Rockyweb uses the popup provider's own controls when it can, otherwise clicks the right buttons, then unlocks pages left unscrollable. Strict mode always rejects everything optional.

custom mode

What the custom default mode turns on. Changing one of these while another mode is the default makes custom the default, starting from that mode's settings.

websites

Sites with their own mode, domain choices or hidden elements. A setting covers the whole domain, so shop.example.com uses example.com.

trackers

Third-party domains Rocky has sniffed out. One that follows you to 3 or more websites is learned: balanced strips its cookies and strict blocks it. Payment, login and captcha providers are never restricted automatically, and shared CDNs at most lose their cookies.

reset learning

Forget every learned tracker. Your own choices for individual domains are kept.

page changes

Elements you hid with the picker (popup, gear, hide an element) or added here. They stay hidden on that site in every mode except off.

described changes and page fixes

Changes you described in the toolbar, and page fixes you applied or Rockyweb applied for you. Switching one off puts the page, and any setting it changed, back as it was.
